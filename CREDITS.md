The assets are used as they are, apart from scaling and orientation set in `game/view/creep_art.gd`, `game/view/tower_art.gd` and `game/view/terrain_view.gd`. Trees, rocks and mountains are recoloured by our own shaders (`game/view/shaders/foliage.gdshader` and `rock.gdshader`). The plateau, cliffs, land, river, grass tufts, flowers, portal arches, tower foundations, elemental spires and crystals are procedural.

## Audio

| Files | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `game/assets/audio/*.ogg` (all sound effects, both music layers and the menu music) | Synthesized from sine waves and noise by [`scripts/make_sfx.py`](scripts/make_sfx.py) | Mazeborn TD contributors | CC0 1.0 |

No sampled or third-party audio is used. The CC0 packs we wanted (Kenney, OpenGameArt) could not be downloaded from the build environment, so every sound is generated by the script, which is deterministic: run `scripts/make_sfx.py` (numpy, and ffmpeg with libvorbis for OGG) to recreate or tweak them.
